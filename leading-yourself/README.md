# leading-yourself

A coaching prompt that helps you uncover and write down the three foundations of leading yourself: **purpose**, **vision**, and **values**.

Paste `leading-yourself-prompt.md` into Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor exactly as it is. Don't fill anything in first. The AI asks one question at a time, pulls evidence from your real experiences, and turns your answers into a personal self-leadership guide.

## What it covers

The prompt runs in six stages, in order:

- **Choose the depth.** Pick Focused, Standard, or Deep. If you're unsure, Standard is the recommended default.
- **Purpose.** Who you are, why you exist, and the contribution you want to make. Built from stories about times you felt most alive, useful, or drawn to improve something.
- **Vision.** Who and where you want to be in three years and in ten years. Uses the Best Possible Self exercise to describe an ordinary day, not just achievements.
- **Values.** The five qualities you choose to live by. Found through stories first, then a card sort, then tested against real behaviour.
- **Assess.** Rate your current clarity on each pillar from 1 to 10 and name what would move it one point higher.
- **Produce the guide.** A finished Markdown document with your approved statements, the evidence behind them, an analysis of strengths and tensions, and a 90-day alignment experiment.

## Usage

1. Copy the full contents of `leading-yourself-prompt.md`.
2. Paste it into any AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, etc.).
3. Answer one question at a time. Say "I don't know" or skip anything you'd rather not answer, and the AI will try a smaller prompt instead.
4. Review and approve the draft wording for each pillar. The AI won't write the final guide until you have.
5. Save the result. If the AI can create files, it writes `leading-yourself.md`. Otherwise it shows the full Markdown on screen for you to copy.

Revisit the guide after meaningful experiences. Your first version is a working draft, not a permanent answer.

## What it is not

This is a structured reflection tool, not therapy, a clinical treatment, or a psychological assessment. The 1–10 ratings are a personal baseline, not a validated score.

- [Four-decade review of self-leadership research and training](https://doi.org/10.3390/admsci11010025)
- [Mental contrasting with implementation intentions (WOOP)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4106484/)
