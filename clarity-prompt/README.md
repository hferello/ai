# clarity-prompt

A lead magnet prompt for Post 14 — *"Cheap building doesn't hide weak thinking. It exposes it."*

When someone comments **EXPOSE** on the post, DM them the contents of `prompt.md`. They paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, or Cursor, describe their product, and get an honest verdict on whether it has a point of view.

## What it tests

Three checks, in order:

- **Style** — can the product's point of view be said in one sentence?
- **Coherence** — does that point of view hold on every screen?
- **Problem** — who is it for, and what breaks for them if it never exists?

## Usage

1. Copy the prompt block from `prompt.md`
2. Paste into any AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, etc.)
3. Replace the placeholder with a product description, URL, or screenshots
4. Read the verdict — and act on it
