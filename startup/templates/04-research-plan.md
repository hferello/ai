## Interview Script

1. **Warm-up** (2 min): Build rapport. No right or wrong answers.
2. **Current behaviour** (10 min): "Walk me through the last time you experienced [problem]."
3. **Pain & frequency** (5 min): "How often? What does it cost you?"
4. **Workarounds** (5 min): "What have you tried to solve this?"
5. **Ideal outcome** (3 min): "If this vanished tomorrow, what changes?"
6. **Wrap-up** (2 min): "Anything I should have asked but didn't?"

## Interview Techniques

- **Five Whys:** If an answer is vague, ask "why?" up to five times. The root cause hides deeper.
- **Mom Test rules:** No pitching. No leading. No hypotheticals. Ask about the past.

## Key Things to Listen For

- Emotional language (frustration, anger, relief)
- Specific dollar amounts or time lost
- Workarounds they've built
- Competitors they've tried and abandoned
