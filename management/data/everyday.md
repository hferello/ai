# Data example: Everyday (personal, home, life admin)

**The Okonkwo family** — planning a two-week summer trip to Portugal with two kids and a grandmother who needs regular rest stops.

Files: `docs/context.md` · `briefs/portugal-itinerary.md`

---

## Context file

```markdown
# Project Context

> **One-liner:** A two-week Portugal trip that works for an 8-year-old, a teenager, and Grandma — without feeling like a military schedule.

## Mission

Give the family a trip they'll talk about for years: good food, some culture, time on the beach, and enough downtime that nobody comes home exhausted. The win is shared memories, not ticking off every landmark.

## Principles

- One big thing per day, not three. Mornings for activities, afternoons free or slow.
- Grandma comes first on walking and heat — if she can't do it comfortably, we skip it.
- Kids get a say. Each person picks one "must do" for the whole trip.
- Book the non-negotiables early; leave room to wander.

## Must-Haves

1. Flights and accommodation booked before we plan day-by-day details.
2. A rough day-by-day itinerary everyone can read on their phone.
3. Restaurant reservations for at least three special dinners.
4. A simple packing list by person.

## Constraints

- Travel dates: July 14–28 (fixed — school holidays).
- Travellers: two adults, kids aged 8 and 14, Grandma (72, walks with a stick, fine on flat ground).
- Budget: about £4,500 total excluding spending money (flights already £1,800).
- Base in Lisbon first week, Algarve coast second week — flights home from Faro.
- No hire car in Lisbon; OK to rent in the Algarve for beach days.

## Out of Scope

- Adventure sports, long hikes, or late-night nightlife.
- Luxury hotels or private tours.
- Side trips to Spain or Morocco.
- Detailed budget tracking spreadsheets — a rough daily spend guide is enough.

## Standards

- Itinerary in a shared Google Doc — one section per day, bullet points not essays.
- Times are approximate ("morning", "late afternoon") unless it's a booked slot.
- Flag anything that needs booking with a link or "book by [date]" note.
- Celsius for weather; distances in km; prices in euros with a rough £ equivalent.

## Success Criteria

- Every traveller can name their one "must do" and it's on the plan.
- No day with more than 90 minutes of solid walking for Grandma's routes.
- Total planned spend stays within budget before pocket money.
- Kids and Grandma both say they'd go again — asked honestly before we book non-refundable bits.
```

## Work brief: Portugal day-by-day itinerary

```markdown
# Work Brief: Two-week Portugal itinerary

## 1. Overview

A day-by-day plan for July 14–28: Lisbon (7 nights), travel day, Algarve coast (6 nights), fly home from Faro. Flights and hotels are booked — this brief is for the daily plan, reservations worth making, and a packing list.

## 2. Goals

- A readable itinerary the whole family can follow without asking Mum every hour.
- Balance culture, beach, and rest — especially for Grandma.
- Surface what to book now vs what can wait until we're there.

## 3. Who this is for

- **Grandma:** needs seated breaks, shade, and toilets nearby; happy with cafés and views.
- **Age 8 (Amara):** animals, castles, ice cream, not long museum rooms.
- **Age 14 (Jordan):** some independence OK with mates on the beach; hates "baby" activities.
- **Adults:** good food, one proper cultural hit per city, not racing between sights.

## 4. Requirements

1. One section per day: date, location, morning / afternoon / evening suggestion.
2. Mark Grandma-friendly routes explicitly; flag steps, hills, or long standing.
3. Include each person's "must do" from the context file (Amara: dolphins; Jordan: surf lesson; Grandma: fado night; Dad: pasteis de nata crawl; Mum: tile museum).
4. Three dinner reservations with neighbourhood and vibe noted — not just names.
5. Travel day between Lisbon and Algarve with realistic timing.
6. Short packing list by person (carry-on only for kids).

## 5. Non-Goals (Out of Scope)

- Hour-by-hour schedules.
- Full restaurant reviews — one line on why each pick fits us is enough.
- Travel insurance or visa info (already sorted).

## 6. Practical Considerations

- Lisbon: no car; use metro, trams, and taxis for Grandma on hot days.
- Algarve: car rental picked up day 8 — plan beach days around that.
- July heat: outdoor stuff early or late; indoor or shaded midday when possible.

## 7. Success Metrics

- Mum reads it once and says "I can work with this" — no full rewrite needed.
- Every "must do" appears on the plan exactly once.
- Grandma's longest walking day is under 90 minutes of continuous walking.

## 8. Open Questions

- Sintra as a day trip — too much walking for Grandma, or OK with taxi between stops?
- One shared room setup in Algarve vs adjoining rooms for the teenagers?
```
