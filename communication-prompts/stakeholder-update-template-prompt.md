# Stakeholder Update Template Prompt

Paste this whole thing to Claude (or any AI assistant) as-is — don't fill in anything yourself first. The AI will ask you questions, then build you a stakeholder update, plus a reusable template you can fill in again next time.

---

You are my communication coach. I need to send a project update to stakeholders that increases their awareness of my work, improves their understanding of it, builds their trust in how I'm managing it, and opens the door for them to respond — without turning into a wordy essay.

**Step 1 — Ask me these questions, one at a time, and wait for my answers before moving to the next:**

1. What's the project, and who are the stakeholders receiving this update? (This helps me gauge how much background context they need to actually understand what's going on, versus what they already know.)
2. What's the current status — green, yellow, or red (or an emoji if you'd rather)? Briefly, why that status?
3. In 2–3 sentences, where does the project stand today? Think "breaking news alert," not a full article.
4. What are 2–4 recent accomplishments worth highlighting?
5. Are there any upcoming risks or blockers stakeholders should know about before they become a problem?
6. How do you want to invite feedback or questions — reply to the email, a specific channel, an open office hours slot, something else?

**Step 2 — Once I've answered, give me two things:**

1. **The update itself**, structured as:
   - Status indicator (color or emoji) up top
   - The 2–3 sentence overview, front and center, no scrolling needed
   - Recent accomplishments as a short bullet list
   - Upcoming risks/blockers as a short bullet list
   - A closing line that explicitly opens the door for questions or feedback, using the channel from question 6
2. **A reusable template version** of the same structure, with each section replaced by a short bracketed placeholder (e.g., "[Status: Green/Yellow/Red]," "[2–3 sentence overview]"), so I can reuse this format for future updates without rebuilding it from scratch each time

Keep the tone confident and concise throughout — the goal is something a stakeholder can read in under a minute and still walk away able to explain the project to someone else.
