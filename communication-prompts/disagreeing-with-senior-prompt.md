# Disagreeing With a Senior Prompt

Paste this whole thing to Claude (or any AI assistant) as-is — don't fill in anything yourself first. The AI will ask you questions, then use your answers to script the conversation.

---

You are my communication coach. I need to disagree with someone more senior than me, and I want to do it in a way that leads with respect, acknowledges their broader view, states clearly what I hope to gain and want to avoid, and closes with appreciation regardless of outcome.

**Step 1 — Ask me these questions, one at a time, and wait for my answers before moving to the next:**

1. What's the decision, statement, or plan you disagree with? Describe it.
2. Who is this person to you — their role and how senior they are relative to you?
3. What's your actual concern or alternative idea — what do you want them to understand?
4. What do you hope to gain from this conversation, and what do you want to avoid seeming like? (e.g., "I want to share a concern" / "I don't want to seem resistant to change")
5. Is this already set up as a private, one-on-one conversation? If it started in a public setting, do you need help figuring out how to move it there instead?

**Step 2 — Once I've answered, script the conversation with these parts, in order:**

- **Respect opener** — a line acknowledging their experience or wisdom, said before any pushback (e.g., "You've been doing this a lot longer than I have")
- **Perspective check** — a question inviting their broader view before you push your point (e.g., "Help me understand this from your perspective" or "Do you have a bigger picture that would help me understand this decision?")
- **A reminder to listen and paraphrase** what they say back, before continuing — note that understanding their view doesn't mean agreeing with it
- **Contrast statement** — one line combining what you hope to gain and what you want to avoid, built from your answer to question 4
- **Closing appreciation** — a line expressing thanks that they were willing to listen, regardless of how the decision goes

If the situation started or risks happening in public, also give me a short line I can use in the moment to table it for a private conversation instead.
