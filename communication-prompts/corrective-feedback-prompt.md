# Corrective Feedback Prompt

Paste this whole thing to Claude (or any AI assistant) as-is — don't fill in anything yourself first. The AI will ask you questions, then use your answers to craft the feedback conversation.

---

You are my communication coach. I need to give someone corrective feedback, using the What / Why / How approach: What is descriptive (specific behavior, not personal adjectives), Why is evidence-based (impact, not judgment), and How is action-oriented (support, not just criticism).

**Step 1 — Ask me these questions, one at a time, and wait for my answers before moving to the next:**

1. What happened? Describe the specific behavior or situation — stick to facts and actions, not labels or character judgments.
2. Who is this person to you — a peer, a direct report, or someone above you? Anything about the relationship that matters here?
3. What's the impact of this issue — on you, the team, a project, or an outcome? Is there any concrete evidence (missed deadlines, specific incidents, feedback from others)?
4. What kind of support, resource, or plan could realistically help address this going forward?
5. Do you already have a time and place in mind for this conversation, or do you want suggestions?

**Step 2 — Once I've answered, give me:**

- A short "heads-up" message I can send beforehand to announce that I have feedback to share, so it doesn't land as a surprise (e.g., "I took some notes on X — let me know when's a good time to talk")
- A suggestion for timing and setting, favoring a neutral, one-on-one moment over a public or rushed one
- The core feedback message itself, structured as:
  - **What** — the specific behavior, described neutrally and factually, no personal adjectives
  - **Why** — the impact, backed by the evidence I gave you, not just an opinion
  - **How** — a forward-looking, action-oriented question or offer of support (not just a demand to fix it)
- One line reminding me to stay open to hearing their side, since feedback works best as a conversation, not a verdict

Keep the tone direct but non-adversarial throughout — the goal is to be clear and kind at the same time.
