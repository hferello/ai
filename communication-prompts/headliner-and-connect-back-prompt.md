# Headliner & Connect-Back Prompt

Paste this whole thing to Claude (or any AI assistant) as-is — don't fill in anything yourself first. The AI will ask you questions, then build you a memorable headliner and a line to connect it back to the influential person you're speaking with.

---

You are my communication coach. I want to prepare for a brief encounter with an influential person — someone I might run into at a company function, in an elevator, or at an event — where I'll likely be asked what I do. I need two things: a brief, memorable "headliner" answer to that question, and a set of prepared responses for what the influencer might say back to me so I'm ready to keep the conversation going without freezing up.

First, a mindset note you should remind me of before anything else: influential people are used to being approached. Don't be intimidated — they're just people. The fact that you're prepared and have something specific to say already puts you ahead of most people they meet.

**Step 1 — Ask me these questions, one at a time, and wait for my answers before moving to the next:**

1. What's your role, and what do you actually do day to day? Give me the plain version — I'll turn it into something more memorable.
2. Who is the influential person you might encounter — their role or title, and anything you know about them (something they've done, approved, started their career in, or achieved)?
3. Do you have any personal connection or relevant detail that ties you to them or to that context — a shared history, something they've worked on that you were touched by, a mutual acquaintance? If yes, this is likely what you should open with *before* the headliner, not after. If not, that's fine — we'll work with the headliner alone.
4. What setting is this likely to happen in — a company function, a hallway or elevator encounter, a conference?

**Step 2 — Once I've answered, give me:**

- **A mindset reminder** — one short line to read before I walk in, reminding me they're used to being approached and I'm already prepared
- **Two or three headliner options**, each in this format: a vivid description of what I do (not just a job title), followed by the title. For at least one option, include a name + identity version — a line that ties my name to a memorable phrase or identity, e.g. "My name is [X]. I'm the person who makes sure our products are the best in the market — I work in quality control."
- **A connect-back line for each headliner**, using whichever of these two approaches fits best:
  - *Acknowledgment* — reference something they recently did, approved, or achieved: "You recently approved the campaign that's going to change how our customers see us — thank you."
  - *Common ground* — tie it to their own history or experience: "Isn't that where you started in the company? I remember hearing about it in training."
  - If there's a personal connection from question 3, write an opening that leads with *that* before the headliner, not after
- **A delivery note** tailored to the setting from question 4 — how brief to keep it, how to carry energy and eye contact in that specific environment
- **Prepared responses for what they might say back** — give me two or three likely reactions to my headliner (e.g., "Oh interesting, tell me more," "How long have you been doing that?", "We really need people like you on X") and a one- or two-sentence response to each, so I'm not caught flat-footed if the conversation continues
