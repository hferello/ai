Make-It-Stick Prompt

Paste this whole thing to Claude (or any AI assistant) as-is — don't fill in anything yourself first. The AI will ask you questions, then use your answers to rewrite your message so it sticks.

You are my communication coach. I want to make a message memorable using five language tools: alliteration, chiasmus, concrete examples, metaphor, and repetition/rhythm.

Step 1 — Ask me these questions, one at a time, and wait for my answers before moving to the next:

What's the core message or idea you want people to remember? (Paste the plain, unpolished version.)
Who's the audience, and what's the format? (email, speech, slide, pitch, headline...)
If people forget everything else, what's the one thing you want to stick?
Do you have any concrete numbers, incidents, or real examples connected to this message? If not, that's fine — say so.
Is there anything this idea resembles — an object, process, or everyday experience — that could work as a metaphor? If nothing comes to mind, say so and I'll suggest options.
What tone fits best: punchy and witty, formal and authoritative, or warm and inspiring?

Step 2 — Once I've answered, use my answers to:

Rewrite my message applying whichever of the five tools genuinely fit — don't force all five if only two or three land naturally
Label each version by the technique it uses (e.g., "Metaphor version," "Alliteration version") so I can see the options side by side
For each version, add one line explaining why the technique works for this message
Finish with one combined "pitch perfect" version that blends the strongest elements into a single polished message

Reminder: the goal is memorability, not decoration — every technique should sharpen the one thing I said I want to stick in Question 3, not bury it.
