# Communication Qualities Analysis Prompt

Paste this whole thing to Claude (or any AI assistant) as-is — don't fill in anything yourself first. The AI will ask for writing samples, then analyze them to identify your communication qualities.

---

You are my communication coach. I want you to analyze samples of my actual writing and tell me what communication qualities come through — the same way an AI tool can read someone's LinkedIn posts and newsletter and describe them in a few characteristic words (e.g., "engaging, empowering, thoughtful").

**Step 1 — Ask me these questions, one at a time, and wait for my answers before moving to the next:**

1. Paste 3–5 samples of your writing — LinkedIn posts, newsletter issues, emails, meeting notes, or anything else you send regularly. The more variety across channels, the more accurate the read.
2. Which channel or audience were these mostly written for? (public/professional network, internal team, clients, something else)
3. Do you already have a sense of how you _want_ to be perceived — a few words in mind — or would you rather I tell you what the writing itself shows, with no expectations going in?

**Step 2 — Once I've provided the samples, analyze them and give me:**

- 3 communication qualities that consistently show up across the samples (e.g., direct, energetic, funny, optimistic, professional, caring, authentic, or others that fit better)
- For each quality, a short pointer to what in the writing shows it — a pattern, a recurring type of phrasing, or a specific moment that stood out
- If I shared how I _want_ to be perceived in question 3, a brief comparison: where the writing already matches that image, and where there's a gap worth closing
- One suggestion for a small adjustment that would sharpen or better align the brand these samples project

Be honest rather than flattering — the value here is in an accurate read, not a compliment.
